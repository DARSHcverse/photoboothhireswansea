## What that price includes

Everything needed to run the booth. Specifically:

- Unlimited photos and instant prints, with a copy for everyone in the shot
- A custom photo strip design with your names, date and colours
- A professional DBS-checked attendant for the whole hire
- A standard backdrop and a full set of event props
- Delivery, setup and pack-down
- A custom start screen

Setup happens 60 to 90 minutes before your start time and is **not** counted in your hire hours. Your paid time begins when the booth opens to guests.

Longer packages add more: USB and an online gallery from three hours, a traditional guest book and your choice of attendant from four.

## What is not included

Being straight about this matters more than the headline number.

**Travel outside our usual area.** Swansea, Cardiff, Bridgend, Neath, Port Talbot, Llanelli and the Vale are covered as standard. Further out, we will tell you the travel cost before you book — never after.

**Premium backdrops and neon signs.** A standard backdrop is included. Sequin, floral and specialist backdrops are add-ons. The [backdrop page](/backdrop) shows what is available.

**Idle time, sometimes.** If a venue needs the booth built hours before it opens — a room flip, a restricted access window — that is usually fine and usually free. For unusually long gaps we would discuss it in advance.

That is the whole list. There is no per-print charge, no setup fee, no attendant fee and no charge for the props.

## Which length do people actually book?

**Three hours** is the most common, and for good reason. Two hours goes quickly once guests realise the booth is there — the queue builds in the first twenty minutes and you lose a chunk of your window to the rush.

**Four hours** suits weddings particularly well, because it covers the gap between the meal finishing and the dance floor emptying, and it includes the guest book.

**Two hours** works properly for shorter events with a clear window: a corporate drinks reception, a school event with a fixed finish.

**Five hours** is for events that genuinely run long. If your event finishes at eleven, five hours of booth is more than you need.

## Why cheaper quotes exist

You will find cheaper photo booth hire in South Wales. It is worth understanding what usually differs.

**No attendant.** The single biggest cost in any booth hire is a person standing next to it for four hours. Drop-and-collect booths are cheaper because nobody is there when the paper runs out or the queue stalls.

**Prints charged separately.** A low headline price can mean unlimited sessions but only one print per group, with extra copies charged on the night.

**Setup inside your hire time.** If the booth arrives at your start time and takes 45 minutes to build, a three-hour booking is really two and a quarter.

**Older equipment.** Print quality varies far more than people expect, and you only find out afterwards.

None of that makes a cheaper supplier wrong — a drop-off booth at a casual party may be exactly right. It just means comparing headline prices alone will mislead you. The [questions worth asking any supplier](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-booking-photo-booth-hire) covers how to check.
