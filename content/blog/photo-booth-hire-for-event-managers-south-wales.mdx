If you run events for a living, you are not booking a photo booth. You are managing a supplier — one of fifteen — and what you actually need is for that supplier to turn up, behave professionally in front of your client, and never become your problem.

This page is for planners, agencies, venue coordinators and anyone else handing over a finished event to a client.

## Why go through us rather than a different booth each time

**Loyalty rates on volume.** If you are placing several events with us across a season, you are not paying list price. The more of your calendar we cover, the better the rate — and it is agreed up front for the whole block, not haggled event by event.

**Priority on your dates.** Saturdays in June, July and December go first, and they go early. Partners get first refusal on dates before they are released, which means the backlog of events you are holding can actually be resourced instead of scrambled.

**One point of contact.** You get a direct number and you deal with the same person every time. No call centre, no being handed to whoever picked up.

**One invoice.** Consolidated billing across multiple events, on terms, instead of a separate payment run for every booking.

**A supplier who knows your venues.** We work across Swansea, Cardiff, Bridgend, Neath, Port Talbot, Llanelli and the Vale regularly. Load-in quirks, lift access, which rooms have a socket where you need one — that knowledge saves you a site visit.

1. **Send us your calendar.** Even provisional dates. We will tell you honestly what we can hold and what is already gone.
2. **We quote the block.** One rate across the set of events, with the loyalty discount applied.
3. **We hold the dates.** Provisionally, while your client confirms.
4. **You confirm event by event.** Individual events firm up as your clients sign off, against the agreed rate.
5. **We invoice together.** Monthly or per-block, whichever suits your finance process.

There is no minimum commitment to have the conversation, and no charge for holding provisional dates while your clients decide.
