# Instagram Voice Guide

Derived from real captions written by Shan. The caption bot reads this file.
If the voice drifts, fix it here rather than in the bot prompt.

## Structure of a post

1. **Hook line** — short, punchy, one emoji. Often a claim or a contrast.
   - "Be our first in South Wales 🏴📸"
   - "Meet the camera behind every shot 📷"
   - "Everything included. No surprises. 🖨️✨"
2. **Two or three short lines** setting up the idea. Heavy line breaks — never
   a dense paragraph. It has to scan on a phone.
3. **An emoji-prefixed list** when there are features to show.
4. **A short closing argument** — why this matters to the reader.
5. **Availability line**, near-verbatim:
   "Now booking from March 2027 across Swansea, Cardiff and South Wales 🏴"
6. **CTA**, near-verbatim: "Check your date → link in bio 👆"
7. **4–6 hashtags**, CamelCase, each with a `#`.

## Voice rules

- Short sentences. Fragments are fine and used deliberately.
- Repetition for emphasis is a signature move:
  "Every time. Not sometimes. Every time."
- Confident comparison, never naming a competitor by name:
  "While most photo booths use iPads and tablets — we use a Canon EOS R100."
- British English: colour, personalised, organise.
- Plain "we", never "the team at Photo Booth Hire Swansea".
- Em dashes for asides, used often.
- No exclamation-mark spam. One per caption at most.

## Emoji

Functional, not decorative. Each bullet gets an emoji that matches its point.
Recurring set: 📸 🖨️ ✨ 🎨 👤 💷 🏴 📷 👆 💾 📖 🪄 🖼️ 📱 🌐

🏴 always follows "South Wales". 👆 always ends the CTA line.

## Hashtags

Four to six, CamelCase, always prefixed with `#`. Core set:

`#PhotoBoothHire` `#SwanseaWedding` `#SouthWales` `#WalesWedding`
`#SwanseaEvents` `#CardiffWedding` `#PhotoBooth` `#WeddingPlanning`

Rotate two or three topical ones in: `#CanonR100`, `#PromNight`,
`#ChristmasParty`, `#CorporateEvents`.

> A past caption shipped `WeddingPlanning WalesWedding` with no `#`. Always
> check every hashtag actually has one.

## Facts that may be stated

Only these. Anything else must be verified against the site first.

- Prices from **£250** (Selfie Pod / Enclosed, 2hr); Glam Booth from **£300**
- Canon EOS R100 mirrorless camera, professional studio lighting
- Unlimited photos, instant prints, custom strip design, props, backdrop
- DBS-checked attendant, included in every package
- USB digital images; online gallery on 3hr+; guest book and extra print on 4hr+
- Covers Swansea, Cardiff, Bridgend, Neath, Port Talbot, Llanelli, the Vale
- Setup 60–90 minutes before start, not counted in hire time
- Previously ran a photo booth business in Melbourne, Australia
- Launching March 2027; booking open now for 2027 dates

## Never

- Invent reviews, testimonials, customer names, ratings or booking counts.
  There are no published reviews yet.
- Invent scarcity ("only 2 slots left"). Factual scarcity is fine:
  "Summer Saturdays fill first."
- Quote a price not in `app/packages/page.tsx`.
- Name a competitor.
- Claim awards, press coverage or partnerships.

## Post types that work

- **Feature spotlight** — one inclusion, explained properly (the Canon post)
- **Transparency** — what is included, what things cost (the pricing post)
- **Comparison** — our approach vs the common approach
- **Booth type** — Selfie Pod vs Glam vs Enclosed
- **Seasonal** — Christmas parties, prom season, summer weddings
- **Behind the scenes** — strip design, setup, backdrops
- **Blog promotion** — one takeaway from a post, link in bio
